⚠️ Weather reminders

No live weather service is connected. The 48–72hr outbreak warning in AgriAlly's concept requires real weather data and a validated prediction model.

Prevention alerts

Common-sense prompts you can use right now.

💧
Root-zone moisture checkBefore the next irrigation

Use a sensor or check soil at root depth rather than relying on surface colour alone.

🍃
Scout for diseaseTwice weekly in risk periods

Look for spreading spots, unusual yellowing, pests and wilting.

🌧️
Rain-aware wateringBefore running the pump

Check a trusted local forecast and avoid unnecessary irrigation before rainfall.
